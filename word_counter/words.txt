The city buzzed with life as morning sunlight spilled across the quiet streets. Vendors opened their shops, their voices echoing through the narrow alleys. The smell of fresh bread, roasted coffee, and street food filled the air, mixing into a comforting rhythm that belonged only to this place. People hurried to work, children laughed on their way to school, and the sound of buses, bicycles, and footsteps created a symphony of motion. The city was alive, alive in every sense of the word. It breathed, it moved, it remembered. The city was not just a place; it was a feeling, a heartbeat, a story written and rewritten every single day. And even when night fell, the city did not sleep. The city never really slept.
